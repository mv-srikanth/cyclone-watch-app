Live data

Satellite and radar refresh every 5–10 minutes, JTWC every 10 minutes, GDACS every 30 minutes, and model runs are checked every 20 minutes (new runs arrive four times a day). Keep this page open and it updates itself.

Official warnings & sources

India Meteorological Department (IMD) — official warnings RSMC New Delhi — tropical cyclone bulletins IMD Doppler radar — Visakhapatnam GDACS — global disaster alerts Joint Typhoon Warning Center (JTWC) Google DeepMind Weather Lab (sign-in; own terms)

Model data (best resolution first): ECMWF IFS HRES 9 km, UK Met Office 10 km, DWD ICON 13 km, NOAA GFS, ECMWF AIFS 28 km, plus ECMWF ENS and NOAA GEFS ensembles, via Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0). Satellite: NASA GIBS (Himawari-9, IMERG, VIIRS, Blue Marble), EUMETSAT (Meteosat-9 IODC). Radar: RainViewer. Advisories: JTWC and GDACS. The stage shown is estimated from JTWC winds; IMD issues the official classification and name. Storm centres are traced in your browser from each model's sea-level pressure (±30 km); the cone is where ⅔ of ensemble runs put the centre.

Not an official forecast. For warnings, evacuation and fishing advisories follow IMD and your state disaster management authority.
